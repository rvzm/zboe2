Zombie Biohazard Outbreak Experiment 2

A persistent online zombie survival RPG built for the browser.

zboe2 started as an IRC-based bot, named simply zboe which had no acronymic meaning, that simply informed chatters when zombies appeared, allowing them to issue a command (originally, the bot used ".shoot" as a chat command, and ".reload) to interact. Eventually the IRC bot got a small shop where users could buy ammo, but thats about as far as it went. That was in 2012. Now, it's 2026, and zboe2 has been born (circia 2024 ish) as something new: Zombie Biohazard Outbreak Experiment. When this project started zboe became zboe2, to signify it's "rebirth" and gained an acronymic meaning. However, we started as "Zombie Biohazard Occult Experiment 2", because i was struggling to come up with what the O in zbOe could mean, but always knew that "Occult" wasn't quite right. Then it hit me: Outbreak.

🌎 About the Game

ZBOE combines classic browser RPG mechanics with modern progression systems.

Players venture into dangerous zombie-infested locations, gather resources, improve their equipment, craft better gear, complete quests, and slowly rebuild humanity's chances of survival.
